# PC Guard Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026
**Publisher:** Alyssa Baker
**Contact:** pcguardapp@outlook.com

PC Guard is a Windows app that locks your computer when someone other than
you uses it while you are away. This policy explains what data PC Guard uses
and where it goes.

## Summary

All PC Guard data stays on your computer. PC Guard does not upload, sell, or
share your data, and it has no accounts, analytics, or advertising.

## Data PC Guard uses

- **Camera images.** PC Guard uses your webcam to detect faces and compare
  them to the people you have enrolled. Processing happens on your computer.
  When PC Guard raises an alert because someone is not recognized, it saves a
  snapshot image from the camera to a folder on your computer so you can
  review it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Face data.** When you enroll yourself or a trusted person, PC Guard
  stores a numeric face template on your computer. It is not a photo and
  cannot be turned back into one. Enrolled people can be removed from the
  dashboard at any time.
- **Keyboard and mouse activity.** PC Guard detects that a key was pressed or
  the mouse was clicked, so it can check who is at the PC. It does not record
  which keys you press or what you type.
- **Activity log.** PC Guard records events, such as an alert or a lock, in a
  local log you can view in the dashboard.

## What PC Guard does not do

- It does not send any data to the publisher or any third party.
- It does not require an account or an internet connection.
- It does not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Biometric data

Face templates and snapshots are used only to decide whether the person at
the PC is enrolled and to show you who triggered an alert. They are stored
only on your device. Anyone who enrolls another person should get that
person's consent first.

## Deleting your data

Remove enrolled people from the PC Guard dashboard. Snapshots are stored in a
folder you can open from the PC Guard tray menu ("Open Snapshots Folder"),
where you can delete them at any time.

## Children

PC Guard is not directed at children under 13.
